Marg Sarathi – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026  ·  App package: com.mintu.lifeguide

Marg Sarathi (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a free Android app. You type a problem from your life, and the app replies with guidance, three practical steps and verses from the Bhagavad Gita and Chanakya Niti. This policy explains what happens to the information you give the app. We wrote it in plain language on purpose.

In short

  • There is no account, no login, and the app does not ask for your name, phone number or email.
  • The text you type is sent over the internet to our server and then to Google’s Gemini AI so that it can write your guidance. We do not keep a copy of it.
  • Saved answers stay only on your phone. You can delete them at any time inside the app.
  • If you tap “Report this answer”, we receive that answer and your reason, not what you typed. Reports are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • There are no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell or share your information for marketing.

3. Google’s Gemini AI

The guidance is written by Google’s Gemini AI. Google receives the text you type and processes it to produce the reply.

During the test period we use Google’s free Gemini service. On the free service, Google may use the text it receives to improve its products. Because of this, please do not type names, phone numbers, addresses, account numbers or other details that could identify you or other people. You can describe your situation in general words.

Google handles that information under its own terms and privacy policy. You can read them on Google’s website.

4. How we use information

We use the information only to give you guidance, to show urgent help numbers when your message suggests danger, to keep the app working, and to review reports so that we can improve the safety and quality of answers. We do not sell your information. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not build profiles of users.

5. Who else receives information

Only the service providers that make the app work: Cloudflare (hosting of our server and report storage) and Google (the Gemini AI). We may also share information if the law requires us to.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Saved answers: delete one answer, or everything, inside the app. Uninstalling the app also removes them.
  • Not using the app: nothing is collected until you tap “Get guidance”.
  • Questions or requests: write to [YOUR EMAIL]. Because we do not know who you are and do not store what you type, we may not be able to find a particular report that you made, but we will do our best to help.

7. Security

Requests between the app and our server use an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. No system is perfectly secure, so please follow the advice above and avoid typing details that identify you.

8. Age

The app is meant for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the app, please contact us.

9. Important: this is not professional advice

The guidance in the app is general, written by an AI, and can contain mistakes. It is not medical, legal, financial or mental-health advice, and it does not replace a doctor, lawyer, counsellor or other qualified person. If you or someone else is in danger, call 112. For mental-health support in India you can call Tele-MANAS on 14416 (or 1800-891-4416), free, 24 hours.
